My name is Quynh Anh Nguyen. I am a PhD student in the Medical Image Analysis Laboratory, Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, at Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology (TUAT), Tokyo, Japan, since April 2024, under the supervision of Professor Akinobu Shimizu. I received the B.S. degree in Information Networking for Innovation and Design from Toyo University, Japan, in March 2022, and the M.E. degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from TUAT, Tokyo, Japan, in March 2024.
